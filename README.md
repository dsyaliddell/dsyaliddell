# Hii ! 🦕

I'm a **junior web developer** and a final-year **Web Development** student -
but honestly, **design has always come first for me**. Code is my brush.

My journey started with **HTML and CSS** and at first, I didn't really enjoy it.
I even doubted my choice and wondered if I should have picked something else.
Then one day I discovered **award-winning, immersive websites** - the kind that
feel alive and look nothing like ordinary pages.
They felt like **art**, not templates. That's the moment everything clicked for me. I've realized I want to be the person who designs them too.

Right now I'm learning **JavaScript**, and my next goal is **GSAP** to create
cinematic.

I'm at the very beginning of my journey. **No big experience yet** just a lot
of taste, a good eye for design, and the will to keep going. 🐨

🌴_______________

## 🐋 My Philosophy

It should catch the eye, spark emotion, and leave an aftertaste.
Minimalism, bold shapes, cinematic feel, and character.

My references are sites where:

- **Design** comes first, and code is the tool to bring it to life
- **Aesthetics** are never sacrificed for functionality
- **Animation** tells a story, not just "decorates"
- **The user** feels the atmosphere from the very first second
